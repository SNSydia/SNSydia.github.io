# Next Line

**Version 0.1.0 · first version, 7 November 2026**

Live app: https://snsydia.github.io/next-line/

A mobile-first progression app for mountain bikers: what can I ride, and what's next? It is the sibling of [Crash Map](https://snsydia.github.io/crash-map/) and works fully offline.

## Tabs

- **Today**: a plan for the day at your park (a warm-up, a trail you know, and today's new one), in your riding style, with notices from your Crash Map log (easing back after an injury, fatigue).
- **Park**: every trail at the park as a ticklist (not ridden, ridden, ridden clean with the date), with readiness and filters. Open a trail to rate a ride (confidence 1 to 5, conditions, clean or not) and to see its crashes from Crash Map.
- **Skills**: the skills ladder (tried, landed, consistent) for the trail features: berms, rollers, drops, jumps, roots, rock gardens and more.
- **Goals**: ride a trail clean, or reach a level on a skill; goals complete themselves.
- **Me**: your profile, the rider builder, the theme (Trail Dusk, Morning, Blossom) and export or import of your data.

At the end of the day, **Reflect in 30 s** records the trails you rode, anything new, and the conditions.

## Your data

Everything stays on your phone (in the browser's storage for this site). Next Line reads Crash Map's log on the same site, read-only, and never changes it. Facts from Crash Map are shown in Crash Map's green.

This folder is the built app (from `apps/next-line/` in the suite repository); don't edit it by hand.
